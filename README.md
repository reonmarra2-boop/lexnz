## What is LexNZ?

LexNZ is a free, all-in-one legal study tool built specifically for New Zealand law students. It covers 8 areas of NZ law, includes AI-powered research grounded in NZ case law and statutes, and provides structured learning tools built around the frameworks NZ law schools actually use — PEEAL and ILAC.

It was built by a NZ law student who knows what it's like to be thrown into a fact scenario at 11pm with a submission due tomorrow and wish there was one place to find the right cases, structure the argument, and check the law.

---

## Features

### 🔍 AI Legal Research
Ask any NZ legal question and get structured answers grounded in NZ case law, statutes, and legal principles — with proper citations. Toggle persuasive authority from England, Australia, Canada, Ireland, and the US.

### 📝 PEEAL Argument Builder
Build legal arguments using the PEEAL framework (Point · Explanation · Evidence · Application · Link). AI reviews your argument, suggests missing NZ citations, and flags weaknesses. Export to PDF.

### 🎓 ILAC Learning Tool
Three modes to master the Issue · Law · Application · Conclusion method:
- **Learn** — interactive accordion with worked NZ examples
- **Build** — write your own ILAC with AI review
- **Practice** — 6 real NZ-style fact scenarios, marked out of 10 with a model answer

### 🧠 Quiz & Flashcards
- 12 multiple-choice questions across all areas of NZ law — filterable by topic
- 12 flip-card flashcards covering key principles, cases, and definitions
- Works fully offline — no internet required

### 📚 Case Library
Pre-loaded with key NZ and persuasive precedents. Add your own cases as you study — saved permanently to your browser.

### 📖 Legal Glossary
20+ plain-English definitions of legal terms and Latin maxims, including te reo Māori legal concepts like tino rangatiratanga and kāwanatanga.

### 🔖 Bookmarks & PDF Export
Bookmark any research result, export to print-ready PDF, and access your saved research any time.

## Getting Started

### Option 1 — Download and open locally
1. Download `LexNZ.html`
2. Open it in Chrome, Edge, or Safari
3. Enter your [Anthropic API key](https://console.anthropic.com) in the API key bar to unlock AI features
4. Quiz, Flashcards, Glossary, and Case Library work offline without a key

### Option 2 — Visit the hosted version
Go to **[lexnz.vercel.app](https://lexnz.vercel.app)** — no download needed.
