Argument Frameworks

The frameworks NZ law runs on

Both PEEAL and ILAC built in — so you can use the right structure for every question, whether it's an essay argument or a problem scenario.

P
PEEAL Framework
Point · Explanation · Evidence · Application · Link
Use PEEAL for essay and analytical questions where you're building a structured argument about the law. Each section is guided, AI-reviewed, and PDF-exportable. Best for opinion and analytical writing.
I
ILAC Method
Issue · Law · Application · Conclusion
Use ILAC for problem-based questions where you're advising a party on a fact scenario — which is most NZ law exam questions. Practice on 6 real scenarios, get marked out of 10 with a model answer.

Built by a New Zealand law student who knows what it's like to be thrown into a fact scenario at 11pm with a submission due tomorrow — and wishing there was one place to find the right cases, structure the argument, and check the law.
